UNcommonVCU launch calendar

What to post and what to do with the ads, every day from Oct 16 to Dec 14. Launch is Mon Oct 26, as soon as Evan approves. Updated Oct 16, 2026.

How to use it

  • Find today. Post is what goes on Instagram. The gray line under it is its folder in the Social Team folder (open the week, then the day), and the italic line is what the post says.
  • Two posts a day, three on the big days (launch, opener eve, the opener, NC Central, the last day). The label says morning, midday or evening.
  • Nothing posts until Evan approves, so launch (Mon Oct 26 at the earliest) waits on his OK. needs Evan’s OK marks the posts that use an Athletics photo or a player’s own words; if he approves only part of it, see “If launch slips” in How to.
  • Posts in the library that aren’t on the calendar are listed under Spares at the end. Use one any day something feels off.
  • Every feed post also goes in Stories. Ads only shows up on days something changes.

What we’re posting from

  • Coldon’s set of 3: the 60-second coach interview (horizontal, for the website) and its two vertical cuts, “An Invitation” and “Fan Attendance”.
  • Tiny Mic set of 3: Gabby’s player interviews. Nine of the stills quote them too (Ada, Ann, Daija and Nicole, word for word).
  • The stills library: the post batch in Social Outputs (tweet cards, text and photo carousels, photo dumps, quizzes, countdown, gameday and final-score posts).
  • That’s everything. The ads use the same pieces, videos first.

The money, start to finish ($200)

  • Mon Oct 26 Launch. Google starts at $2.40 a day. Meta launch ads start: $25 over 10 days (about $2.50 a day).
  • Wed Nov 4 Meta launch ads end at 11:59 PM ($25 spent). Google: remove the “Nov 3” headlines.
  • Thu Nov 5 Meta game ads start: $75 through NC Central tip-off on Nov 16 (12 days, about $6.25 a day).
  • Thu Nov 12 Google goes up to $4.80 a day for the five days before NC Central.
  • Mon Nov 16 Meta ends at tip-off ($100 spent). Google turns on the “Watch” ad group.
  • Tue Nov 17 Google back down to $2.40 a day.
  • Mon Nov 30 Google stops at 11:59 PM (about $98 spent). Everything is off.

Why Meta runs so long: it starts with no data, and it needs time to learn who clicks. Long, steady runs learn better than short bursts, so the $25 runs 10 days and the $75 runs 12, the shortest runs that still give it time to learn. Every edit restarts the learning, so build each set of ads once and leave it alone.

Why the Google budget doubles Nov 12–16: Meta can put most of its money on the NC Central game, but Google only spends when people search, so it can’t. Raising its daily limit for the five days before the game is the closest thing. Running $2.40 the rest of the time keeps the total at about $100.

How to

The steps the calendar points to.

If launch slips past Mon Oct 26

  1. Launch needs two things: Evan’s OK and a working Instagram login. Google doesn’t need either, so it starts Oct 26 regardless.
  2. Launch day is always the three posts on Oct 26 (Who we are, Meet coach Banbury, Day One). Post them on the real first day, then pick the calendar up on that date.
  3. Days before launch are skipped. Their posts mostly point at Nov 3, so they only fit before the opener; if one fits, swap it in for a later pre-opener post.
  4. Meta: if the launch ads can’t start by Oct 26, don’t run a separate short launch flight (under 10 days doesn’t give Meta time to learn). Run one $100 set from the start day to NC Central tip-off (Nov 16) with all the ads: the launch videos and the game ads. Build it once, then leave it alone.
  5. If Evan approves only some items: posts marked “needs Evan’s OK” use an Athletics photo, a player’s own words, or both. Post only what he approved and skip the rest that day.

The approval request to Evan (Coldon sends it, cc Grayson; full version with a draft email in Group Handoffs › UNcommonVCU_Evan_Approval_Request_Coldon.pdf)

  1. The Athletics photos we have access to. Our social posts are built from them, credited “VCU Athletics” on every slide.
  2. The official Athletics headshots, for the player pages on the site. Once approved: Appearance → Customize → Player photos → tick “Show player headshots” → Publish.
  3. The players’ questionnaire answers (the Google Form), quoted in posts in their own words.
  4. Coldon’s Coach Banbury interview (60 seconds, for the site) and its two vertical cuts, “An Invitation” and “Fan Attendance”.
  5. Gabby’s Tiny Mic player interviews (three pieces). His OK on these, plus the players’ answers, also covers the 9 stills that quote the Tiny Mic answers word for word.
  6. Everything else in the post batch is our own words or public facts and doesn’t need approval.

The day Evan approves

  1. Site headshots: Appearance → Customize → Player photos → tick “Show player headshots” → Publish.
  2. Coach interview on the site: Media → Add New → upload Coldon’s 60-second horizontal interview → copy its File URL. Then The Board → Settings → Coach interview → paste it into Video URL → check that “Detected” recognises it → tick “Show on the site” → Save.
  3. Add the “add when approved” lines from HANDOFF/pivot-page-copy.md (How it works and the FAQ). Pages → open the page → Edit → paste → Update.
  4. Meta: if the launch ads aren’t running yet, publish them today (see “If launch slips” for the dates).

  2. Negative keywords: Tools → Shared library → Exclusion lists → + → name it “UNcommonVCU negatives” → paste the list from the Google Ads package (Ads Team › Packages).
  3. Conversions: Goals → + Create conversion action → Import → Google Analytics 4 → tick uvb_account_claimed, uvb_checkin_success, uvb_ticket_click. Make account_claimed Primary, the other two Secondary.
  4. New campaign → Search → name it “ue-launch”. Untick search partners and the Display Network. Turn off the AI Max options.
