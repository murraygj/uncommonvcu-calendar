UNcommonVCU launch calendar

What to post and what to do with the ads, every day from Oct 7 to Dec 14. Updated Oct 7, 2026.

How to use it

  • Find today. Post is what goes on Instagram. The gray line under it is its folder in the Social Team folder (open the week, then the day), and the italic line is what the post says.
  • Some days get one post, some two, a few three. When there’s more than one, the label says morning, midday or evening.
  • If a post says needs Evan’s OK and we don’t have it yet, do what the line under it says (skip it, or post the backup). Backups run through Oct 25. If the OK still isn’t in by then, tell Claude and we re-plan the rest.
  • Posts in the library that aren’t on the calendar are listed under Spares at the end. Use one any day something feels off.
  • Every feed post also goes in Stories. Ads only shows up on days something changes.

What we’re posting from

  • Coldon’s set of 3: the 60-second coach interview (horizontal, for the website) and its two vertical cuts, “An Invitation” and “Fan Attendance”.
  • Tiny Mic set of 3: Gabby’s player interviews.
  • The stills library: the post batch in Social Outputs (tweet cards, text and photo carousels, photo dumps, quizzes, countdown, gameday and final-score posts).
  • That’s everything. The ads use the same pieces, videos first.

The money, start to finish ($200)

  • Mon Oct 12 Google starts at $1.80 a day. Meta launch ads start once Evan OKs the videos (by Oct 16 at the latest): $25 spread to Nov 3.
  • Tue Nov 3 Meta launch ads end at the opener’s tip-off ($25 spent).
  • Wed Nov 4 Meta game ads start: $75 through Nov 16 (about $5.80 a day). Google: remove the “Nov 3” headlines.
  • Thu Nov 12 Google goes up to $3.60 a day for the five days before NC Central.
  • Mon Nov 16 Meta ends at tip-off ($100 spent). Google turns on the “Watch” ad group.
  • Tue Nov 17 Google back down to $1.80 a day.
  • Mon Nov 30 Google stops at 11:59 PM (about $99 spent). Everything is off.

Why Meta runs so long: it starts with no data, and it needs time to learn who clicks. Long, steady runs learn better than short bursts, so the $25 stretches over three weeks and the $75 over 13 days. Every edit restarts the learning, so build each set of ads once and leave it alone.

Why the Google budget doubles Nov 12–16: Meta can put most of its money on the NC Central game, but Google only spends when people search, so it can’t. Raising its daily limit for the five days before the game is the closest thing. Running $1.80 the rest of the time keeps the total at about $100.

  3. Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → uncommonvcu-fixes-20261007-1806.zip → Install → Activate. Read the green box: it lists every change (email contact line on About and Privacy, $10 seat price, the Peppas line, player bios, Yoast fields). Anything under “do by hand” has the exact wording. Then Deactivate → Delete.
  4. The Board → Accounts → Reset for relaunch → type RESET → confirm. This clears the test accounts.
  5. Plugins → UNcommonVCU Site Gate → Deactivate.
  6. Open uncommonvcu.com in a private window. It should load with no password.
  7. Search Console → URL Inspection → paste https://www.uncommonvcu.com/ → Request indexing. Do the same for /team/, /gameday/ and /play/.
  8. Looker Studio dashboard → set the date range to start today.
